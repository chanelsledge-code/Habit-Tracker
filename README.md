# 🌟 ADHD-Friendly Habit Tracker

A beautiful, encouraging habit tracker designed specifically for ADHD brains. Track daily habits, weekly rituals, and monthly milestones with grace periods, celebrations, and zero shame!

## 🚀 How to Use

### Quick Start
1. **Open the app**: Simply double-click `index.html` to open it in your web browser
2. **Bookmark it**: Add it to your browser bookmarks or home screen for quick access
3. **Start tracking**: Check off your habits as you complete them!

### On Mobile
- Navigate to the file on your phone
- Open `index.html` in your mobile browser (Safari, Chrome, etc.)
- Add to home screen for app-like experience:
  - **iOS**: Tap Share → Add to Home Screen
  - **Android**: Tap Menu → Add to Home Screen

## ✨ Features

### Four Main Tabs

**TODAY** - Your daily view
- Non-negotiable habits (meditation, Oura ring, reading)
- Additional habits with easy checkboxes
- Current streak tracker with grace period system
- Weekly completion percentage

**WEEK** - Weekly overview
- 7-day visual grid showing your progress
- Weekly rituals (Monday Oura review, Sunday setup)
- Teaching schedule (Tue/Wed/Thu)
- Instagram posts counter

**MONTH** - Monthly milestones
- Weight progress with positive framing
- Yoga membership growth
- Revenue tracking
- Books read
- Instagram followers
- Debt reduction
- Oura ring metrics (Sleep, HRV, Readiness)
- Update button (highlighted on 1st of month)

**PROGRESS** - Long-term view
- Badges earned
- Year-at-a-glance stats
- Trend graphs for weight and membership
- Personal records

## 🎯 ADHD-Friendly Features

### Grace Period System
- **Miss 1 day**: Your streak stays alive! Shown in yellow as a gentle reminder
- **Miss 2 consecutive days**: Fresh start with encouraging "Starting fresh!" message
- **No shame language** - ever!

### Celebration System
- 🎊 Hit 80%+ daily habits in a week: Confetti animation!
- 🏆 Complete all weekly rituals: Special badge
- 📊 Update monthly goals on time: "Data Queen!" badge
- ✨ Made progress: Specific encouraging messages highlighting your wins

### Quarterly Auto-Adjustment
The app automatically knows what quarter you're in and shows the right targets:
- **Q1 (Jan-Mar)**: Initial goals
- **Q2 (Apr-Jun)**: Increased targets + strength training appears
- **Q3 (Jul-Sep)**: Higher targets
- **Q4 (Oct-Dec)**: Maintenance mode + podcast tracking (from May)

## 🎨 Design

- **Warm earth tones**: Terracotta, sage green, cream, and gold
- **No red colors**: Gray for unchecked items (not triggering!)
- **Smooth animations**: Satisfying but subtle
- **Mobile-first**: Perfect for phone use
- **Touch-friendly**: Easy to tap on mobile

## 💾 Data Storage

- All your data is saved **locally** in your browser
- Works **offline** - no internet needed
- Persists between sessions
- Private - nothing is sent to any server
